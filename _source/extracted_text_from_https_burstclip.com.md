## Everything you need in one guided workflow

Start from text, your own voice, or an existing video. BurstClip walks you through each step until your story is ready to share.

### AI Script Builder

Describe your idea, answer guided questions, and generate scene-ready scripts for stories, promotions, education, and news.

### Voice & Narration

Generate AI narration with multiple voices and tones, or record your own voice and auto-transcribe captions.

### Timeline Editor

Add AI or stock visuals, trim clips, apply GPU-powered transitions, background music, and styled captions.
